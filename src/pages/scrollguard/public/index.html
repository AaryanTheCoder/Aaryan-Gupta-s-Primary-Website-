01
When a guarded feed opens

First, make an intentional choice.

ScrollGuard covers the feed with a calm pause screen. The feed cannot scroll behind it until you decide what to do.

✓ Best choice

Protect my time

Choose this when you want to leave the feed.

  • Just quit closes the current tab immediately.
  • Start focus mode closes the tab and blocks every enabled distracting feed for your chosen time.
◷ Intentional break

Continue with a limit

Choose the finish line before you start scrolling.

  • By time gives you a countdown in minutes.
  • By videos counts clips when their web address changes.
02 During a limited break

Keep an eye on the small progress badge.

A badge stays in the top-right corner of the feed. It shows the remaining time or your video count. Press End if you decide to finish early.

03 Focus mode

Distracting feeds close without a warning.

Start focus mode from the pause screen or the extension toolbar. Until the timer finishes, opening an enabled feed immediately closes its tab.

Need to stop early? Click the ScrollGuard icon and choose End focus early.

Focus is protected 24:18 remaining
04 Toolbar settings

Choose which feeds are protected.

Click the ScrollGuard toolbar icon to turn individual site rules on or off. Informative websites and normal YouTube videos are not part of the guarded list.

Video counter tip

Some websites do not change their URL for every swipe. On those sites, a time limit is more reliable.

Your data stays local

ScrollGuard has no account, analytics, or cloud server. Your settings and totals remain inside Chrome on this computer.

Changed the code?

Open chrome://extensions and press ScrollGuard’s reload button to use the new version.
